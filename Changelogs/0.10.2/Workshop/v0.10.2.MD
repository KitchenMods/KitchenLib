[h1]Release Notes[/h1]

- `MaterialUtils.AssignMaterialsByNames()` is no longer an extension method, fixing ambiguous `AssignMaterialsByNames()` calls in files using both `KitchenLib.Utils` and `KitchenLib.Materials`.
- `MaterialUtils.AssignMaterialsByNames()` can no longer be compiled against; use `MaterialManager.AssignMaterialsByNames()` instead. Mods already compiled against it continue to work.
